Create a complete Markdown interview preparation brief. Use web search for current company information and cite each time-sensitive or company-specific claim with a working source URL.

Required sections:
1. Evidence boundary and research date.
2. Company overview: business model, products/services, customers, revenue logic and recent developments.
3. Relevant business workflow and where the role contributes.
4. JD decomposition: responsibilities, hard requirements, preferred signals and likely evaluation criteria.
5. Resume-to-role map using only confirmed profile evidence.
6. Likely company/role questions with answer directions.
7. Likely resume follow-ups for each major experience, including weak points and verification questions.
8. A concise mock interview script and final preparation checklist.
9. Source ledger with URL, publisher, publication/update date and what each source supports.

Never treat a missing source as evidence that something is false. Separate verified facts, reasonable inference and unknowns. Prefer official company, regulator, exchange and authoritative primary sources.
